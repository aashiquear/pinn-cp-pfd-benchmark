## Content (checked by opening the workbooks)

One sheet, two header rows. `... v1.1.xlsx`: 2187 data rows, 51 columns; the older file: 1987 data rows (6 alloys).
Columns: Material, author, year, link to paper, printer manufacturer and model, laser power (W), laser speed (mm/s), layer thickness (um), hatch spacing (um),
beam size (um), focus offset, scan strategy, layer rotation, island/stripe size, build plate temperature, linear/area/volumetric energy density,
density measurement method, `Density/Consolidation (%)`, error, heat treatment, hardness, tensile, creep and fatigue data.
Rows per alloy (v1.1): TI6Al4V 697, IN718 320, 316L 317, AlSi10Mg 311, Hastelloy X 185, IN625 157, high-entropy and glassy alloys 200.
Rows with a numeric density value: 1642 overall; 316L 312 (308 with power and speed, 25 papers, density 65-99.9 %, machines SLM250, Concept Laser M2, EOS M280, ...);
IN718 233 (34 papers); Hastelloy X 185 (122 with power and speed); IN625 84.
Missing values are written as `-` or left empty; one IN718 density value is 0 (not checked against its source).

## Caveats

Defect quantity is relative density (consolidation, %), from mixed methods (Archimedes, optical, XCT; method often not given). No pore-type label and
no cracking column (the word "crack" does not occur in the workbook). Values were transcribed by the compilers from papers; not verified here.
